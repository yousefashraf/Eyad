Frequently Asked
Common Questions
What does a personalized program include?
+
Every program includes a fully customized training plan with periodized programming, a macro-calculated nutrition strategy, supplement recommendations, and ongoing coaching support with regular check-ins.
How many days per week will I train?
+
Training frequency depends on your goals, schedule, and recovery capacity. Most clients train 3-5 days per week, but programs can be designed for 3 days if needed.
Do you offer Ramadan-specific plans?
+
Yes. Ramadan plans are fully adapted to fasting windows — adjusting meal timing, training schedules, hydration strategies, and caloric distribution to maintain progress during the holy month.
What level of experience do I need?
+
None. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced lifter, every program is calibrated to your current level and designed to progress you safely and effectively.
How is the nutrition plan structured?
+
Nutrition plans are macro-based with flexible food selection. You receive calculated targets for protein, carbs, and fat — with meal ideas and food lists tailored to your preferences and lifestyle.
Can I reach you for support between check-ins?
+
Absolutely. You have direct messaging access for questions, form checks, and guidance between scheduled check-ins. I'm here to support you throughout the process.
